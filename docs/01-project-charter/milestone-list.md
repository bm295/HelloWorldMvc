# Milestone List - MilkCO FnB Management API

## Timeline Baseline
The timeline below is aligned to the charter date (March 4, 2026) and intended for MVP stabilization through pilot.

| Milestone ID | Milestone | Target Date | Status | Owner | Exit Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Charter package completed | March 4, 2026 | Completed | PM/BA | Charter and linked outputs are documented in repo |
| M2 | Sponsor charter approval | March 8, 2026 | Planned | Sponsor + PM/BA | Sponsor approves scope, objectives, and constraints |
| M3 | API stabilization complete | March 15, 2026 | Planned | Technical Lead | Core endpoints validated; critical defects resolved |
| M4 | UAT execution complete | March 22, 2026 | Planned | QA + Operations | UAT scenarios pass for inventory, orders, payments |
| M5 | Pilot go-live decision | March 27, 2026 | Planned | Sponsor + PM/BA | Go/no-go review completed with risk and readiness checklist |
| M6 | Pilot launch | March 29, 2026 | Planned | Technical Lead + Operations | Pilot environment running and monitored |

## Dependencies
- M2 depends on review of charter outputs.
- M4 depends on M3 stabilization outcomes.
- M6 depends on go-live decision (M5) and no unresolved blocker risks.
