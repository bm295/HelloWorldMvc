# Stakeholder List - MilkCO FnB Management API

## Stakeholder Register (Initial)
| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Engagement Strategy | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Restaurant Owner | Project Sponsor | Business value, budget control, rollout approval | High | Weekly decision meeting; milestone sign-off | PM/BA |
| Operations Manager | Product Owner Proxy | Day-to-day usability, process fit, service speed | High | Weekly backlog and UAT review sessions | PM/BA |
| Cashier Team Representative | End User | Fast order flow and low friction operations | Medium | UAT walkthroughs and workflow feedback | Operations Manager |
| Kitchen/Inventory Representative | End User | Stock accuracy and timely updates | Medium | Inventory scenario testing and exception review | Operations Manager |
| PM/BA | Delivery Lead | Scope, timeline, and risk control | High | Governance cadence, issue escalation, and reporting | Sponsor |
| Technical Lead | Solution Owner | Architecture quality, reliability, release readiness | High | Technical checkpoints and go/no-go input | PM/BA |
| Backend Engineer(s) | Delivery Team | Feature completion and defect resolution | Medium | Sprint-level task tracking and code review | Technical Lead |
| QA Lead | Quality Owner | Functional coverage and regression protection | Medium | Test planning, defect triage, acceptance evidence | PM/BA |
| DevOps/IT Support | Deployment Owner | Environment stability and recoverability | Medium | Deployment runbook checks and health monitoring | Technical Lead |
| Finance/Accounting Representative | Business Stakeholder | Payment traceability and reconciliation | Medium | Data validation review before pilot sign-off | PM/BA |

## Communication Cadence
- Weekly: Sponsor and core delivery checkpoint.
- Twice weekly (during UAT): defect triage and readiness review.
- Ad hoc (within 24 hours): escalation for blocker risks or go-live impact issues.
